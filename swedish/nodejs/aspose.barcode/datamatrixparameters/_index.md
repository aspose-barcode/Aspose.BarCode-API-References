### getMacroCharacters() {#getmacrocharacters}

ISO/IEC 16022 5.2.4.7 Makrotecken 11.3 Protokoll för makrotecken i den första positionen (ECC 200 only) Makro Characters 05 and 06 values are used to obtain more compact encoding in special modes. Can be used only with DataMatrixEccType.ECC_200 or DataMatrixEccType.ECC_AUTO. Cannot be used with EncodeTypes.GS_1_DATA_MATRIX Default value: MacroCharacter.NONE.
