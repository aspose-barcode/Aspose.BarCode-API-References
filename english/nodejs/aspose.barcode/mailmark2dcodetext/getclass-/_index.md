## getClass_()

Identifies the class of the item. Valid Values: '1' - 1C (Retail) '2' - 2C (Retail) '3' - Economy (Retail) '5' - Deffered (Retail) '8' - Premium (Network Access) '9' - Standard (Network Access)

**Returns:** class of the item
