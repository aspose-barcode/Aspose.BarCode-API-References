## setBarcodeType()

Gets or sets barcode type. HIBC LIC codetext can be encoded using HIBCCode39LIC, HIBCCode128LIC, HIBCAztecLIC, HIBCDataMatrixLIC and HIBCQRLIC encode types. Default value: HIBCCode39LIC.

**Returns:** Barcode type.
